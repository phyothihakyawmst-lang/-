First In, First Out (FIFO) Principle

📖 What is a Queue?

A Queue is a linear data structure that follows the FIFO (First-In, First-Out) principle. Think of it like a line of people waiting: the first person to join the line is the first one served (Dequeue), and new people join at the back of the line (Enqueue). The Front index tracks the element to be removed, while the Rear index tracks the last added element.
